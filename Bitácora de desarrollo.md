## **3. Detailed Description of Developed Modules**

### **A. Organization, Registration, and Brainstorming (Morning Session)**

- **Logistics:** The team established a collaborative working environment and internal communication channels to coordinate progress in real time.
    
- **NASA Research:** Official NASA data, mission guidelines, and technical requirements were reviewed to ensure alignment with the challenge's objectives.
    
- **J.A.R.V.I.M. Project Proposals:** Ideas from all six team members were consolidated into a unified solution architecture focused on interactive space exploration.
    

### **B. Technical Development and Educational Content (Afternoon Session)**

- **Platform Translation:** Standardization of technical terminology and cultural adaptation of interface content to ensure clarity, accessibility, and usability in both Spanish and English.
    
- **J.A.R.V.I.M. Platform Programming:** Implementation of improvements to navigation, interaction logic, and mission data visualization.
    
- **Moon and Mars Mission Design:** Development of progressive mission scenarios based on real environmental conditions, including gravity, terrain, available resources, and survival challenges on both lunar and Martian surfaces.
    
- **Glossary and Educational Resources:** Creation of a quick-reference section containing essential definitions related to astronomy and aerospace engineering, together with direct links to verified NASA sources.
    
- **Progression, Rewards, and Global Leaderboard:** Design of gamification mechanics to encourage learning through unlockable achievements, reward systems, progression levels, and a global participant ranking.
    

### **C. Audiovisual Production and Final Submission (Evening Session)**

- **Pre-production and Recording (8:00 – 9:30 p.m.):** Development of the main presentation message, covering the problem, ASTRA's proposed solution, its potential impact, and a functional demonstration. Recording of the audiovisual material.
    
- **Video Editing and Rendering (9:30 – 10:30 p.m.):** Assembly of the final video, audio adjustments, inclusion of J.A.R.V.I.M. team credits, and export in the format required by the competition platform.
